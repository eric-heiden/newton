Add `SensorCamera.Intrinsics` for calibrated pinhole cameras with OpenCV or RealSense inverse Brown-Conrady distortion: `project()` maps world points to OpenCV image coordinates and forward depth, `unproject()`, `unproject_to_depth()`, and `unproject_to_plane()` map image coordinates to rays, points, or plane intersections, `resize()` rescales the calibration, and `compute_camera_rays()` builds the matching ray bundle for `SensorCamera.update()`.
