Add `SolverBase.check_world_values()`, which raises when a solver would not use per-world values of a model attribute after `notify_model_changed()`. `SolverMuJoCo` raises for construction-only and unread attributes, joint target gains of DOFs without a joint-target actuator, actuator parameters of `JOINT_TARGET` actuators, and `shape_scale` of shapes whose MuJoCo assets come from the first world.
